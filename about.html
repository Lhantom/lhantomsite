About

I paint privately, in a small studio in North America,
silently communing with oil paint to capture evanescent memories:
a glance, a shadow, a curve
that slip away from us too soon…

Layer by layer, I caress these moments,
so they may be savored, slowly,
by you…

Should a moment of your own
deserve to be kept,
I welcome your inquiry,
in confidence.
